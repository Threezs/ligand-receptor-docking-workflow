SPP1 pocket definition
======================
Receptor: SPP1_2_model2_rigid.pdbqt (mature SPP1, chain A)
Pocket anchor: RGD motif Arg159-Gly160-Asp161 in the selected model_2
Center (Angstrom): x=-17.2, y=-52.6, z=19.8
Box size (Angstrom): 30 x 30 x 30

Rationale: RGD159-161 is the conserved osteopontin integrin-recognition site. The docking box is centered on the heavy-atom centroid of residues 159-161 with a 30 A margin to sample nearby side-chain and surface contacts. This is a functional-site docking box, not a claim of a crystallographically resolved small-molecule cavity; SPP1 is largely disordered and lacks a full-length experimental crystal structure.

The same box is used for all ligands for comparability.
